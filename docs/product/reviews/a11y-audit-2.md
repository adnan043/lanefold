# Accessibility audit, round 2 (WCAG 2.2 AA, from code)

Static read only. Round-1 items not repeated. Line numbers are from the current tree.

## Top 10
1. **Single-key shortcuts cannot be turned off or remapped** (2.1.4; High). `src/app/shortcuts.tsx:62-86`: bare `/`, `n`, `?` and `g`+letter fire whenever focus is not in a field. Speech-input and screen-reader users will trigger them by accident. No setting exists (grep in `src/app/settings/` finds none). Fix: a "Keyboard shortcuts" switch in Settings > Appearance (stored in prefs.ts, default on) that makes `onKey` return early; or require a modifier. Keep Ctrl+K.
2. **No forced-colors support anywhere** (1.4.11, 2.4.7; High). No `@media (forced-colors: active)` in `src/styles`, `src/app` or `accents.css`. The "contrast" theme is a custom palette and does not engage forced colors. Under Windows High Contrast the accent swatch dots (`lf-swatch-dot`, `appearance.tsx:63`), the selected state, the switch and checked-state fills, WIP and priority chips, the resize handle hover and the `--accent-soft` focus glow vanish (background and box-shadow are dropped). Fix: add one forced-colors block that gives selected, checked, and focus states `outline`/`border` using `Highlight`, `CanvasText`, `ButtonText`, plus `forced-color-adjust: none` on the swatch dots.
3. **Focus indicator removed from the card resizer** (2.4.7, 2.4.13; High). `src/app/cards/cards.css:98-101`: `.cd-resize:focus-visible` sets `outline: none` and relies on a faint `--accent-soft` background, usually under 3:1, and absent in forced colors. Fix: `outline: 2px solid var(--accent); outline-offset: -2px`.
4. **Card title focus ring removed** (2.4.7, 2.4.11; Med). `src/app/boards/polish.css:24-25` `.lf-card-title:focus-visible { outline: none }`, and the card-panel field ring `cards.css:67,100` uses only border plus soft glow. Verify the replacement ring (the card `:focus-within`) is at least 3:1 and not clipped by lane overflow. Same pattern at `chat.css:387` (composer box) and `global.css:196,202`. Fix: keep a transparent `outline: 2px solid transparent` as `global.css:1527` does, so forced colors show it. The round-1 item 8 is only partly fixed: 2 of ~9 places have the transparent fallback.
5. **Toast "Dismiss" label is hard-coded English** (3.1.1/3.1.2 and RTL i18n; Med). `src/portal/ui/overlay.tsx:293` `label="Dismiss"`. Fix: pass a translated label (`t('common.dismiss')`). Toasts with an action also pause only on hover or focus inside; a danger toast still auto-dismisses (2.2.1). Check `sticky` is set for errors with an action, such as the plan-limit toast. The toast regions are now correctly split (round 1 #5 verified: polite status and assertive alert, pause on hover/focus/hidden, 2 s floor).
6. **Card panel focus handling relies on timers** (2.4.3; Med). `card-panel.tsx:302` (60 ms) and `:313` (90 ms) race with the shell's h1 focus (`shell.tsx`). On a slow device focus can land on the `<h1>` and stay there, and the check at `:312` then restores only if focus is on body or h1. The opener is read at first render (`:290-293`): if the panel opens from a deep link, or in Safari, where buttons are not focused on click, `opener` is null and the fallback lookup works only for cards in the DOM. Fix: have the shell skip its h1 focus when the route is the panel (`staticData.keepFocus`) and drop the timers.
7. **Accent swatches: selected state is a 14 px check in a colour dot, with no visible focus rule checked** (1.4.1, 1.4.11, 2.4.7; Med). `appearance.tsx:48-69`. The radiogroup and roving tabindex are correct, and the label text is visible, which helps. Need: confirm `.lf-swatch:focus-visible` exists in `settings.css`/`accents.css` (not found in a quick grep of `lf-swatch`), and that the check mark contrasts with every accent (white check on orange or rose accents is risky; test it in `tests/contrast.test.ts`). Fix: add an explicit ring and a 2 px border on the selected swatch.
8. **Shortcut help modal is not announced as a dependency of the trigger and `?` toggles while a menu is open** (2.1.4, 4.1.2; Low-Med). `shortcuts.tsx:60-64`: the `?` branch runs before the "dialog or menu open" guard, so it fires over any open dialog (a rename form, say) when focus is not in a field, and `armed` state for `g` is stale across route changes. Fix: move the guard above the `?` branch except when the shortcuts modal itself is open. Also announce when `g` arms ("Go to: b, m, i or c") through a polite status.
9. **Chat live region verified, one gap** (4.1.3; Low). Round 1 #3 and #4 hold: `announce.ts` counts only incoming, non-deleted messages newer than the baseline, and `conversation-view.tsx:142` uses a hidden `role="status" aria-atomic`. `composer.tsx:124` has a hidden hint `role="status"` that is rendered in every composer instance, which means several live regions per page (board chat, card comments). Fix: render the hint only while suggestions are open, and do not give it `role="status"` on first mount (an initial mount is often not announced anyway, but updates are noisy).
10. **RTL: physical properties remain** (1.3.2, layout; Low). `src/styles/app.css:299,308` use `translateX(-100%)`/`(100%)` for drawers, and `:1509,1513` use `translateX(±16px)`. These do not flip under `dir=rtl`, so the drawer enters from the wrong edge or the animation reverses. `home.css:610` has `padding-right`; `home.css:82` has `translateX(2px)`. Fix: `[dir='rtl'] &` overrides with a negated value, or a `--dir: -1` variable multiplied in `calc()`; use `padding-inline-end`. The round-1 `text-align:left` fix holds (no physical `text-align` found now). Arrow-key logic in `appearance.tsx` `move` and the card resizer `nudge` should invert Left/Right in RTL (2.1.1 expectations); check both.
