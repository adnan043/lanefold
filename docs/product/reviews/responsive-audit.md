# Responsive audit (static, from CSS and components; not browser-tested)

Top 10, by severity. Paths are under src/.

1. HIGH. app/app.css:468-477 (.lf-dock) and layouts/App.astro:32. Phones (any): no `viewport-fit=cover` and no `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`, so the floating dock sits on the iOS home indicator. Fix: add `viewport-fit=cover` to the viewport meta; `.lf-dock{bottom:calc(.75rem + env(safe-area-inset-bottom))}`; raise `.lf-board` padding-bottom, `.lf-undo-region` bottom (app.css:~490), `.lf-failure-region` (boards/polish.css:~103) and `.chat-layout` padding-block-end (chat/chat.css:505) by the same inset.
2. HIGH. styles/app.css:79 (.app-sidebar height:100vh) and :1817 (.drawer height:100vh). Mobile Safari/Chrome at 320-768: 100vh includes the hidden address bar, so the drawer's bottom items and the drawer's save buttons sit below the visible area. Fix: add `height:100dvh` after each (also `.app-shell` min-height 100vh at :19/:47/:73).
3. HIGH. app/cards/cards.css:11,463-466 (.cd-panel). Phones: the bottom sheet is z-index 30, the same as `.app-scrim` (app.css:316) and `.lf-undo-region` (app.css:454). It also covers the dock, and `inset-block:12vh 0` has the 100vh issue and no bottom safe area. Fix: `inset-block-start:12dvh; padding-block-end:env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`; give `.lf-undo-region` z-index 32 and keep the failure bar at 31. Sidebar (40) over panel (30) is OK.
4. MED. app/app.css:165-168,173,181 (.lf-lanes snap). 320-768: `scroll-snap-type:x proximity` with 85vw lanes and no `scroll-padding`, so the lane's left edge snaps flush to the screen edge and the next lane doesn't peek. Fix: `.lf-lanes{scroll-padding-inline:1rem}` and at <=960px `flex-basis:min(85vw,320px)`. Also fix the desktop 288px lane at 200% zoom (see 8).
5. MED. app/boards/canvas.tsx:62 plus app/app.css:269 (.lf-grip touch-action:none). Phones: the card's TouchSensor (220ms hold, tolerance 8) is fine, but the lane grip's `touch-action:none` blocks horizontal panning when a swipe starts on a lane header, which is a scroll trap in the header strip. Fix: give the lane grip a hold delay too, or limit `touch-action:none` to `.lf-grip[aria-grabbed]` / the dragging state, and add `-webkit-touch-callout:none; user-select:none` on `.lf-card` to stop the iOS long-press menu fighting the hold.
6. MED. app/chat/chat.css:502-523. Phones: the list/conversation switch DOES work (`[data-open]` hides the list; `.chat-back` shows). Issue: `.chat-thread` is `position:absolute; inset:0` inside `.chat-view`, so on <=960px the thread covers the whole conversation, including the composer. Verify the composer isn't inside `.chat-view-body` (if it is, put `inset-block-end` above the composer). Also `.chat-layout{min-height:0}` inherits the 100vh shell chain (see 2).
7. MED. styles/app.css:2415-2420 (.popover max-inline-size calc(100vw - 16px)) and app/app.css:13,55,143 (overflow:hidden ancestors). 320px: popovers are fixed so they are not clipped, but 100vw includes the scrollbar and any max-height is unbounded. Fix: `max-inline-size:calc(100dvw - 16px); max-block-size:calc(100dvh - 16px); overflow:auto`.
8. MED. 200% text / font-scale 150% (app/app.css:173 fixed `288px` lane, `.lf-dock-link` min-width 64px at 11px font, `.cd-panel` `inset-block:60px`). At 150% the dock labels (11px px-based, not rem) don't scale and four or five links at 64px overflow 320px. Fix: `.lf-dock-link{min-width:0;flex:1;font-size:.6875rem;overflow-wrap:anywhere}`; make the lane `flex:0 0 18rem`; panel top `inset-block-start:calc(60px*var(--fs,1))` or `3.75rem`.
9. LOW-MED. RTL (ar/ur/fa). app/app.css:60 `.lf-lanes` is flex, so it correctly flips; `.app-sidebar:dir(rtl)` translateX is handled. Gaps: cards/cards.css:23 `.cd-panel` uses `border-radius:var(--radius-lg) 0 0 var(--radius-lg)` and the RTL override at :27 is correct; `.lf-dock` `inset-inline` is symmetric (fine). Remaining issue: truncated `.lf-switcher-name` (app.css:48), `.chat-pinned-item` and `.chat-card-chip-title` ellipsize with no `title`/tooltip; long unbroken words/URLs or emoji strings in titles overflow cards. Fix: `.lf-card, .chat-msg-body, .cd-title{overflow-wrap:anywhere}` and add `title={text}` to the three truncated spans.
10. LOW. Members/DataTable: styles/app.css:1136 `.table-wrap{overflow-x:auto}` only scrolls sideways; at 320-375 the members table (3 DataTables, app/members/members-screen.tsx:210-242) is a horizontal scroll with no stacked layout, and the scroll region has no `tabindex=0`/label for keyboard users. Fix: add `tabindex="0" role="region" aria-label` in the wrapper, or at <=560px `.data-table thead{display:none}` and `td{display:block}` with `data-label` prefixes. Public pages are fine: pricing (pricing.css:94) and home (home.css:564-600) collapse at 860/560 using minmax(0,1fr). `.drawer width:min(100%,520px)` is OK.

Not found: fixed pixel widths beyond the 288px lane and 640px card panel (capped by `min(...,100vw)`), no horizontal page-level overflow in shell CSS. Dialogs use the top layer, so they win over every z-index above. Sticky headers: no sticky element over focus found, but `.cd-panel` at `inset-block:60px` assumes a 60px top bar (breaks at 150% text). Needs real-device verification of 5 and 6.
