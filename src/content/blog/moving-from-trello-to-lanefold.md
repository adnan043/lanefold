If your team is on Trello and you are curious about Lanefold, you do not have to commit in one step. This guide covers how the ideas map across and a low-risk way to try it, starting with importing a board.

## How the concepts map

| In Trello | In Lanefold |
| --- | --- |
| Workspace | Workspace |
| Board | Board |
| List | Lane |
| Card | Card |

The structure is familiar. What is different is around it: built-in chat, a My Day screen across all your boards, an Inbox, and a keyboard-first way of working.

## Importing a Trello board

Lanefold can import a board from a Trello export file. In Trello, open the board menu, choose Print, export and share, then Export as JSON. In Lanefold, open Settings, then Import and export, and pick that file.

- The file is read in your browser, and you see a preview of what will come over before anything is saved.
- Lists become lanes. Cards, labels, checklists and comments come with them. Attachments arrive as links, and comments are written under your name with the original author mentioned in the text.
- Large boards are sent in small batches, and running an import again does not create duplicates.

Only Trello's JSON export is supported. Check the result on a real board before you rely on it. To take a board out of Lanefold, use its Export menu to download JSON or CSV.

## A low-risk way to switch

1. **Pick one board.** Choose an active project, not your whole archive.
2. **Check its lanes** after the import. Keep your current workflow; do not redesign the process during the move.
3. **Import the board, then tidy it.** Archive or delete finished cards you do not need. Done work can also stay in Trello.
4. **Run both for a week or two.** Let the team try the chat and My Day, and see what they miss.
5. **Move the next board** only if the first one felt better.

## What to decide as a team

- Where chat conversations about work will happen from now on.
- Who is responsible for keeping the board tidy (a [weekly review](/blog/run-a-weekly-review-on-a-board/) helps).
- Whether anything you rely on has no equivalent yet. If so, keep that part where it is for now.

## Be realistic

Lanefold is young. Trello has years of features and integrations we do not have. Try it on a real board, and judge it on whether your team's day gets easier.
