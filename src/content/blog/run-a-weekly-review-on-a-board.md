Boards rot quietly. Cards stop moving, due dates pass, and the board slowly stops matching reality. A short weekly review fixes that. Here is a routine that fits in about twenty minutes. It works on any kanban board, in Lanefold or elsewhere.

## 1. Start from the right side

Begin at the Done lane. Look at what finished this week. Archive what no longer needs to be visible. This takes two minutes and is the only part of the review that feels good, so do it first.

## 2. Walk the in-progress lane

For each card in progress, ask one question: is this actually moving? If it has not changed in a week, one of three things is true:

- It is blocked. Write what it is waiting on, and who can unblock it.
- It is not a priority anymore. Move it back to the backlog.
- It is bigger than it looked. Split it into smaller cards.

## 3. Deal with due dates

Check cards with a due date in the past or the coming week. Either do them, move the date and say why, or remove the date. A due date nobody believes is worse than none.

## 4. Trim the backlog

You do not need to read everything. Look at the top of the backlog and the oldest cards. Close anything you would not do in the next quarter. Closing a card is a decision, not a failure.

## 5. Pick next week

Choose a small number of cards that go into progress. Fewer than you think. Assign each one to a person.

## 6. Write it down

Post a two-line summary in the board's chat: what shipped, what is next, what is blocked. It gives the team a shared picture and gives you a record.

## Make it a habit

Same day, same time, same length. Use My Day afterwards to see your own part of the plan across every board.
