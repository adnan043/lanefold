Most small teams run their work in two places. The plan lives on a board, and the talking happens in a chat app. A card says "waiting on design", and the actual answer is somewhere in a thread nobody can find.

Lanefold puts both in one place: a kanban board and built-in team chat.

## The problem we kept running into

Work gets decided in conversation and tracked on cards. When those are two separate tools, you copy links back and forth, and the context drifts apart. Someone asks "where did we land on this?" and the answer takes ten minutes to dig up.

## What Lanefold does about it

- **Boards with lanes and cards.** Workspace, boards, lanes, cards: the structure you already know from kanban.
- **Chat built in.** Channels for the team and chat tied to a board, so you can talk about work without leaving it.
- **Cards and messages connect.** You can attach a card to a message with # and share a card into a conversation.
- **My Day and Inbox.** One screen for what is on your plate across boards, and one for what needs your attention.
- **Keyboard first.** A command palette and single-key shortcuts for the things you do all day. See the [shortcuts guide](/blog/lanefold-keyboard-shortcuts/).

## Who it is for

Lanefold is aimed at teams of up to about a hundred people who want a calm, fast board and do not want to stitch a chat tool onto it.

## What we are not claiming

Lanefold is new. We would rather tell you what it does today than promise everything a mature tool has. If something you rely on is missing, tell us: that is how we decide what to build next.
